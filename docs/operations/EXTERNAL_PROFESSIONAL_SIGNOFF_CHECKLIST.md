# External Professional Sign-Off Checklist

Status: decision-request package; no approval is implied

Business legal name: `[OWNER TO CONFIRM]`
Business or dealer ID: `[OWNER/ACCOUNTANT TO CONFIRM]`
Pilot scope version: `UNAPPROVED-DRAFT-1`

An uploaded file, meeting, or informal comment is not approval. Each decision needs a dated written response, applicable pilot scope version, evidence, conditions, effective date, and review trigger.

## Israeli counsel

- [ ] Managed-service contracting model and bilingual customer terms.
- [ ] Correct business legal name and registration/dealer details for legal documents.
- [ ] Online cancellation, service-start consent, refunds/credits, remediation, complaints, and electronic cancellation.
- [ ] Provider agreement and actual worker/contractor classification facts.
- [ ] Pilot allowlist, prohibited work, regulated-service/licence rules, and any service/manpower contractor implications.
- [ ] Privacy, consent, address/media handling, rights requests, retention/legal holds, processors/transfers, and incident notification.
- [ ] Electronic quote/terms acceptance evidence.

Required evidence: dated written approval, final document versions/hashes, effective date, conditions, review trigger, professional and firm.

## Israeli accountant or tax adviser

- [ ] Correct legal name, business/dealer ID, entity/dealer status, and required legal document details.
- [ ] VAT treatment and customer-facing price presentation.
- [ ] Deposit/balance invoice and receipt timing.
- [ ] Refund, credit-note, cancellation, and dispute documents.
- [ ] Provider invoice/withholding requirements and payout blockers.
- [ ] Ledger treatment and reconciliation for collected money, VAT, provider payable, fees, refunds, disputes, and rework.
- [ ] Retention/export format and invoice-allocation review process.
- [ ] Unit-economics terms; contribution is not accounting profit.

Required evidence: dated memo, sample pilot transaction documents, provider-document checklist, ledger/reconciliation decisions, review trigger, professional and firm.

## Insurance broker or adviser

- [ ] CleanFixHarish coverage for exact pilot activities and confirmed legal entity.
- [ ] Provider minimum coverage by task/risk class.
- [ ] Subcontracted work, injury, property damage, completed work, tools/materials, and relevant privacy/cyber exposures addressed or excluded.
- [ ] Endorsements, territory, limits, deductibles, exclusions, and subcontractor conditions.
- [ ] Incident notice deadline, evidence duties, and claim contact path.

Required evidence: dated coverage schedule or letter, policy references/dates, exclusions/conditions, claim procedure, renewal owner, professional and firm.

## Status model

`not_started -> packet_ready -> submitted -> questions_open -> conditional | approved | rejected`

History states are `expired` and `superseded`.

- `conditional` remains NO-GO.
- The owner records a professional decision but cannot grant it.
- Open conditions require evidence and professional confirmation.
- Approval for an old scope does not approve a new scope.
- Corrections supersede; they do not erase history.

## Record fields

Gate code, discipline, decision requested, pilot scope version, status, condition status, professional/firm/contact reference, submitted/response/effective/review dates, evidence reference/hash, conditions, owner, notes, and superseded record ID.

Release passes only when every mandatory gate is `approved`, current, matches the active scope, has professional/evidence metadata, and has no open condition.
