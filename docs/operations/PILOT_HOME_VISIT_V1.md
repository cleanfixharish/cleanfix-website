## Proposed paid-pilot allowlist

Only the following proposed narrow tasks may be submitted for professional risk/licensing/insurance classification:

- mounting a customer-supplied item weighing no more than 5 kg, reachable with both feet on the floor, using an existing verified fixing point or an expressly approved fixing method in a dry interior non-utility zone;
- assembling customer-supplied flat-pack furniture whose individual components weigh no more than 25 kg and whose instructions require no structural anchoring, electrical/plumbing connection or two-person lift;
- replacing or adjusting a specifically listed interior cabinet pull, drawer runner or ordinary furniture hinge, without work on locks, security doors/windows, glazing or utility connections; and
- another task only after it is added by name and measurable limits to a new professionally approved version of this allowlist.

Proposed tool/material rule: the performer supplies inspected ordinary hand tools; the customer-supplied item and every fixing/consumable are listed in the written scope. Powered drilling, anchors, wall penetration and customer-supplied substitute parts are prohibited unless a later professionally approved scope version defines the substrate scan, utility-clearance, fixing and evidence requirements.

“Handyman” is never permission for regulated, unknown or higher-risk work. Each task must be classified before price, assignment or booking.

## Actual proposed operating model for professional decision

- CleanFixHarish is intended to be the customer-facing contracting party for a managed job. The customer accepts CleanFixHarish's versioned quote and terms and pays CleanFixHarish only through the exact method/recipient approved by counsel and the accountant. Whether a deposit is permitted remains a written professional decision.
- The proposed sequence is: classified request → immutable scope/checklist → owner-approved versioned quote/terms → customer acceptance → approved payment/deposit record → sequential provider offer → exact-scope eligibility check → owner-confirmed assignment → customer arrival-identity notice and PIN → work/private evidence → quality review → issue/rework path if needed → owner completion and financial-close decisions → provider invoice and approved payout.
- CleanFixHarish prepares the written scope and customer price, confirms the schedule, approves the provider assignment, receives customer communication, reviews documented issues and approves final close.
- A managed provider receives one private assignment with a fixed written payout and invoices CleanFixHarish. The provider may not give the customer a personal number/card, issue a private quote, solicit follow-on work or collect from the customer.
- Provider substitution requires a new exact-scope eligibility check, owner approval and customer notice/confirmation before the substitute receives any private customer data.
- Provider tools/material responsibilities, access requirements and exclusions are written per job before confirmation.
- Customer communication uses the CleanFixHarish channel. Before arrival, the customer receives the performer's first or approved display name, verified photo, CleanFix provider ID, arrival window, one-time PIN and any mandatory licence information.
- Exact address and private media access is least-privilege, assignment-scoped, time-limited, encrypted and audited. Access is revoked on decline, reassignment, cancellation or close.
- Before/after media is kept in private object storage and delivered only with short-lived object-authorized links. Capture excludes unnecessary faces, children, identity documents, screens, keys and security details; EXIF/geolocation is removed. Public use requires separate purpose-specific written consent, and withdrawal stops future use without rewriting required private audit/financial records.
- Incidents, unsafe conditions, material scope differences and customer complaints pause affected work and require owner review.
- Rework, replacement, credit or refund is never automatic; the applicable professional-approved policy and evidence govern the decision.
- No customer collection/refund/credit or provider payout occurs before every activation gate passes. After activation, an approved external/manual transfer must still create the authoritative append-only money record before or atomically with the decision, including amount, currency, type, status and external idempotency/reference without bank/card data, and reconcile to issuer documents. Cash or private transfer is prohibited unless counsel and the accountant approve that exact documented flow. Money cannot financially close a job while recovery or ledger gates fail.
