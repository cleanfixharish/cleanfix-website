CleanFixHarish elevator pitch

Create a very short audio pitch, ideally 60–120 seconds. Make it easy enough for an eight-year-old to understand, but polished enough for a customer, provider, adviser, or investor.

The problem: when a home needs repair, cleaning, maintenance, or another service, the customer often has to find people, repeat the same explanation, compare unclear prices, coordinate time, manage surprise changes, and solve quality problems alone.

The answer: CleanFixHarish is one managed local path for Harish. The customer sends one clear request. CleanFixHarish defines the work in writing, prepares an owner-approved quote, coordinates an approved provider when needed, follows the job, and remains the customer’s contact through quality review.

Why it is different: this is not a phone-number directory or a lowest-price lead auction. The customer gets one responsible brand and a documented process. The provider gets a prepared assignment and a clear agreed payout instead of paying for an uncertain lead. Important price, assignment, refund, payout, and complaint decisions stay under human owner control.

The technology: a bilingual mobile-friendly website, installable web application, protected accounts, separate role-based workspaces, and an owner dashboard keep the request, scope, money, evidence, and next action connected. AI can help explain and draft, but cannot make material promises or financial decisions on its own.

The disciplined opportunity: CleanFixHarish validates real customer outcomes and real job economics before scaling. It does not invent reviews, revenue, traction, or guarantees.

Close with: CleanFixHarish turns a confusing home-service search into one clear local journey—from “something needs fixing” to “the result was checked.” Visit cleanfixharish.co.il to explore, request service, apply as a provider, or discuss the model.
