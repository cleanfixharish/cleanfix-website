CleanFixHarish concept, operating model, and payment rules

The problem

Home-service work is often fragmented. A customer may need to find a provider, explain the same job repeatedly, compare unclear prices, coordinate access, handle unexpected extras, and decide whether the result is acceptable. Providers may receive poor briefs, uncertain schedules, unqualified leads, and payment ambiguity. CleanFixHarish aims to replace that fragmented journey with one accountable local operator.

The operating model

CleanFixHarish is the customer-facing contracting brand for managed services. The customer approves and pays CleanFixHarish. CleanFixHarish owns the written scope, quote, schedule, support, quality review, and remediation path. An approved service provider may perform the assignment for a pre-agreed payout. The provider invoices CleanFixHarish; CleanFixHarish invoices or receipts the customer according to the approved accounting procedure.

The controlled journey is: request, triage, complete information, written scope, provider capacity and payout floor, owner-approved quote, customer acceptance, deposit or payment when required, provider offer, confirmed assignment, arrival verification, work and evidence, quality review, remediation when required, close, provider payout, and follow-up.

What the customer pays

There is no universal fee or invented public price. The customer receives an owner-approved quote for a defined scope. The legally required consumer total, including applicable VAT, must be shown before acceptance. Materials, travel, parking, access limitations, and exclusions must be explicit. A final price is valid only for the written scope. A material change requires a new approved change order.

How a managed provider is paid

The provider does not pay CleanFixHarish for a lead and does not compete in a reverse auction. The provider sees and accepts an exact payout for the assignment and any approved change order. The current planning range is 60–70 percent of the net customer price before VAT, but the actual payout is set per service or job based on skill, scope, time, materials responsibility, and sustainable economics.

The planning model also reserves approximately 4–7 percent for quality or rework, 3–6 percent for payment and direct costs, and 0–8 percent for acquisition or referral when applicable. The target CleanFixHarish contribution before valuing owner time is 20–25 percent, with a warning below 15 percent. A planning minimum contribution is ILS 100–150 per job before owner time. These are validation assumptions, not guaranteed profit. The goal after valuing owner coordination time is a positive contribution of at least 8–12 percent.

The provider payout target, once the process is legally and operationally active, is within two business days after quality close and cleared customer payment. A complaint does not automatically erase the whole provider payout; evidence, notice, and review are required.

Independent referral businesses

Referral-only businesses are separate from managed providers. They contract and collect directly from the customer, use their own identity, and do not receive a CleanFixHarish service guarantee. Paid promotion remains disabled until the core service has at least 20 reviewed jobs and legal and accounting approval. If enabled later, the preferred models are a transparent fixed monthly verified listing or a fixed completed-referral fee with valid consent and attribution. There are no provider auctions, pay-to-rank verification, multi-tier recruitment rewards, or payments for positive reviews.

Why the model can benefit everyone

The customer pays for a managed, documented path rather than an anonymous introduction. The provider receives a prepared assignment and defined payout rather than buying uncertain leads. CleanFixHarish earns only when the quote covers provider payout, direct costs, quality risk, and responsible coordination. The first 20 paid jobs are the validation period: collected money, actual provider payout, direct costs, owner time, issues, rework, refunds, and customer outcomes must be recorded before scaling.

Close with a hook: the important innovation is not another list of professionals. It is a local operating system in which responsibility, scope, money, and quality follow one documented journey.
