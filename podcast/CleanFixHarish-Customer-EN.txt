CleanFixHarish customer guide

Create a warm, concise episode for a Harish resident who needs work done at home and wants to understand what happens next. Aim for five to seven minutes. Do not discuss investor economics in detail. Do not invent response times, availability, reviews, completed work, or guarantees.

Opening hook

Start with this question: when something at home needs attention, should the customer have to become the estimator, scheduler, quality inspector, and dispute manager at the same time?

What CleanFixHarish is

CleanFixHarish offers a managed local service path. It is not simply a list of phone numbers. The company clarifies the request, creates a written scope, prepares an owner-reviewed quote, coordinates an approved provider when needed, keeps the job record organized, and remains the customer’s contact through quality review.

Step one: send a clear request

The responsive bilingual site works on a phone and can be installed as a web application. The customer chooses a service, describes the work, answers relevant scope questions, adds photographs when useful, explains location, access, urgency, and preferred timing, and provides contact and consent information. A successful submission creates one request record and a clear next step.

Step two: clarify before pricing

CleanFixHarish may ask for missing information, better photographs, measurements, or a site review. This is not unnecessary delay; it protects the customer from a confident but unreliable price. No final price should be published before the required scope facts are complete.

Step three: receive a written quote

The quote should explain the exact scope, exclusions, customer price, VAT treatment, deposit if required, materials and travel rules, expiry, and what acceptance means. The customer can accept, request a change, or decline. Acceptance does not falsely mark money as collected or a schedule as guaranteed.

Step four: scheduling and provider identity

After the required payment or deposit step and a capacity review, CleanFixHarish coordinates the schedule and approved provider. The customer receives the safe identity needed for arrival: an approved display identity, arrival window, and any mandatory licence information. Private customer details are released only as needed for the confirmed assignment.

Step five: work without informal surprises

The provider follows the agreed scope and checklist. If a hidden condition or material difference appears, affected work pauses. CleanFixHarish reviews the scope, safety, price, provider payout, and schedule effect. Any customer-price increase requires the customer’s approval before extra work proceeds. No verbal extra should quietly become a surprise invoice.

Step six: evidence and quality close

The provider submits the required completion evidence, but cannot financially close the job alone. CleanFixHarish reviews the checklist and evidence, contacts the customer when appropriate, records the outcome, and opens a documented issue path when needed. Possible responses may include rework, a replacement provider, a credit, a proportional refund, escalation, or a documented decision that the requested item was outside scope.

Privacy and communication

The customer sees only customer-safe information. Internal provider payout, company margin, unrelated customer records, and private internal notes are not public. Spoken accessibility support, keyboard navigation, contrast tools, larger text, reduced motion, and readable transcripts are designed to make the experience easier without extra cost.

What CleanFixHarish honestly promises

CleanFixHarish does not promise unlimited “100 percent satisfaction,” instant availability, or a price before understanding the job. The honest promise is one responsible contact, a clear written process, owner control over important decisions, and a documented review path when something does not match the agreed scope.

Closing call to action

End with: the customer should describe the problem, not manage the entire industry around it. Visit cleanfixharish.co.il, choose the relevant service, and submit a clear request. CleanFixHarish will explain the next step without pretending that an unapproved promise is already confirmed.
