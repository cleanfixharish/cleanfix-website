CleanFixHarish: the concept, the system, and the money

When something breaks at home, why should the customer become a detective, a scheduler, a price expert, and a quality inspector all at once?

That is the problem CleanFixHarish is designed to solve. It is not another list of phone numbers. It is one local, accountable path for home services in Harish.

Here is the simple idea. The customer tells CleanFixHarish what needs attention. The team gathers the missing details, defines the job in writing, prepares a clear quote, and coordinates an approved service provider when one is needed. The work is tracked. Changes are written down. The result is checked. If something does not match the agreed scope, there is one clear place to ask for help.

Think of it like a conductor leading an orchestra. The customer should not need to tell every instrument when to play. CleanFixHarish keeps the people, information, timing, payment, and quality moving through one documented journey.

The journey begins with a request. The customer describes the issue and may add useful photos, measurements, access details, and a preferred time. CleanFixHarish then checks whether enough information exists to define the work. Sometimes the honest next step is another question or a visit, not an instant price.

Once the scope is clear, the owner reviews the quote. The customer sees what is included, what is excluded, the total they must pay, and any important rules about materials, travel, parking, access, deposits, or timing. A price applies to the written scope. If the situation changes in a meaningful way, the extra work and price must be approved before it continues.

After acceptance and any required payment step, an approved provider can receive the assignment. The provider sees the job details and the exact payout offered for that assignment. The provider is not buying a vague lead and is not competing in a race to be the cheapest. They can accept a prepared job with clearer expectations.

During the work, arrival, progress, evidence, and important messages can be recorded. The provider may show that the work is complete, but the provider does not decide alone that the financial journey is closed. CleanFixHarish reviews the agreed checklist and the available evidence, and follows a documented quality process.

How does the money work? There is no invented universal fee. The customer receives a quote for a defined job. From the net price before applicable tax, the plan must cover the provider payout, payment costs, direct costs, quality or rework risk, responsible coordination, and a sustainable contribution for CleanFixHarish.

For early planning, a managed provider payout may often be around sixty to seventy percent of the net customer price before VAT. Other planning ranges may reserve money for payment costs, quality risk, or customer acquisition. These are business-testing assumptions, not public price promises and not guaranteed profit. The actual numbers must be reviewed job by job, then validated using real results from the first paid jobs.

The goal is simple: the customer pays for a managed and documented service, the provider receives a prepared assignment and a defined payout, and CleanFixHarish earns only when the quote responsibly covers the full journey.

Independent businesses that are only advertised or referred are a different category. They use their own identity, contract directly with the customer, and do not automatically receive the same managed-service promise. That difference must always be clear.

The important innovation is not another directory. It is a local operating system in which responsibility, scope, money, and quality follow one path. To start that path, visit cleanfixharish.co.il and describe what your home needs.
