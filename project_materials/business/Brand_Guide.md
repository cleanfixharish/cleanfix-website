# Brand Guide

## Brand Name
CleanFixHarish

## Hebrew Brand Name
קלינפיקס חריש

## Brand Position
CleanFixHarish is a premium local home services brand serving Harish and nearby areas.
The business should feel more refined, calm, and trustworthy than a typical local handyman or cleaning company.
It should be easy to understand, easy to contact, and visually consistent.

## Brand Promise
Practical home services delivered with calm professionalism, fair pricing, and careful attention to detail.

## Core Brand Traits
- Premium
- Local
- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Clean
- Precise
- Practical
- Respectful

## What the Brand Is Not
- Loud
- Cheap-looking
- Flashy
- Messy
- Aggressive
- Gimmicky
- Overhyped
- Overcomplicated

## Customer Perception Goal
When someone sees CleanFixHarish, they should feel:
- this looks trustworthy
- this looks organized
- this feels higher quality
- this looks simple to work with
- this is local and responsive

## Core Message
Premium home services in Harish, with strong handyman support, reliable cleaning, and respectful local service.

## Main Business Focus
The primary service focus is handyman work.
Supporting services include post-renovation cleaning, move-in and move-out cleaning, AC cleaning, and window cleaning.

## Target Audience
- apartment owners
- renters
- families
- busy professionals
- people moving in or out
- people who want reliable local help without dealing with chaos

## Tone of Voice
The voice should be:
- simple
- clear
- polished
- direct
- warm
- calm
- respectful

### Tone examples
Preferred:
- Premium home services in Harish.
- Fast WhatsApp response.
- Fair pricing. Clear communication.
- Handyman, cleaning, and home care with attention to detail.

Avoid:
- We revolutionize your living experience.
- The ultimate all-in-one solution.
- Best-in-class premium excellence.
- Transform your home journey.

## Visual Direction
The brand should feel inspired by luxury restraint rather than luxury display.
The closest reference feeling is the calm digital elegance associated with Rolls-Royce web design, adapted for a practical local service business.

### Visual personality
- minimal
- editorial
- spacious
- elegant
- quiet confidence
- soft premium warmth

## Color Direction
Primary palette direction:
- warm beige
- soft ivory
- pale stone
- charcoal
- deep teal accent

### Suggested palette
- Warm Beige: `#F6F2EB`
- Ivory: `#FBF8F3`
- Stone: `#EDE5DB`
- Charcoal: `#1F1D1A`
- Muted Gray: `#67635D`
- Deep Teal: `#0F3B3E`
- Accent Teal: `#1C5B60`

## Typography Direction
Typography should feel elegant and modern.
Use simple premium sans-serif typography for most interfaces.
Optional serif accents can be used sparingly for display moments, but the default system should stay clean and readable.

### Typography principles
- high readability
- strong spacing
- minimal clutter
- restrained use of display styling
- no trendy decorative fonts

## Logo Direction
The logo should be simple, clean, premium, and scalable.
It should work in:
- website header
- favicon
- social profile image
- future app icon
- invoices or documents

### Logo style guidance
- geometric simplicity
- minimal symbol
- no gradients
- no 3D effects
- no cartoon cues
- no busy icon stacks

## Photography and Image Style
Images should feel:
- realistic
- clean
- premium
- natural
- well lit
- uncluttered

### Preferred imagery
- polished interiors
- precise handyman details
- clean apartments
- bright windows
- calm neutral spaces
- subtle premium tool presence

### Avoid imagery that feels
- low-budget stock
- generic cleaning-ad style
- cluttered and chaotic
- overly staged with fake smiles
- plastic, bright, or noisy

## Language Presentation
The brand is bilingual: Hebrew and English.

### Rules
- Hebrew should be natural and clear.
- English should be polished and simple.
- Customer-facing content should usually present Hebrew first when the audience is local.
- English should support, not overwhelm, the layout.

## Tagline Options
- Premium Home Services in Harish
- Handyman and Home Care, Done Properly
- Premium Local Service, Without the Noise
- Clean. Precise. Local.

## Non-Negotiables
- No fake reviews
- No fake claims
- No exaggerated luxury language
- No messy visuals
- No cheap-looking branding shortcuts
- Handyman must remain a strong visible pillar of the brand
