# WhatsApp and Intake Flows

## Purpose
This file defines how CleanFixHarish should handle customer-first communication, especially through WhatsApp.
The goal is to keep lead capture simple, polite, fast, and easy to manage.

## Communication Principles
- respond quickly
- stay polite and calm
- ask only what is needed first
- avoid long complicated first replies
- keep a premium but human tone
- be easy to understand in Hebrew and English

---

## Main Intake Goals
The first interaction should achieve these things:
1. understand the service needed
2. confirm the location
3. collect contact details
4. determine urgency or timing
5. request photos if relevant
6. move toward a quote or next step

---

## Minimum Intake Information
- full name
- phone number
- area / city
- service type
- preferred timing
- short notes
- photos if needed

---

## Recommended Website Form Fields
- Full name / שם מלא
- Phone / WhatsApp / טלפון
- Type of service / סוג השירות
- Area / אזור
- Preferred timing / מועד מועדף
- Notes / הערות

Optional later:
- upload photos
- budget range
- property type

---

## First WhatsApp Reply Template
### Hebrew
שלום, תודה שפנית לקלינפיקס חריש.
נשמח לעזור.
אפשר לשלוח בבקשה:
1. סוג השירות הדרוש
2. אזור / כתובת כללית
3. תמונות אם יש
4. מתי תרצה שנחזור אליך או נגיע

### English
Hello, thank you for contacting CleanFixHarish.
We’ll be happy to help.
Please send:
1. The service you need
2. Your area / general address
3. Photos if relevant
4. When you would like us to get back to you or come

## Quick Reply: Handyman
### Hebrew
בשמחה. כדי להבין את העבודה, אפשר לשלוח תמונה קצרה של מה שצריך ולכתוב באיזה אזור בחריש מדובר?

### English
Of course. To understand the job, please send a quick photo of what you need and let us know which area in Harish it is.

### Hebrew

Of course. Please let us know if this is post-renovation cleaning or move-in / move-out cleaning, and send a few photos if possible.

## Appointment Confirmation Template
### Hebrew

### English
Thank you, we are set for [date] at [time].
If anything changes or there is something important to know in advance, feel free to update us here on WhatsApp.

## Post-Service Follow-Up
### Hebrew
תודה רבה.
נשמח לשמוע שהכל היה לשביעות רצונך.
אם תרצה, אפשר גם לשלוח כאן חוות דעת קצרה.

### English
Thank you very much.
We hope everything was to your satisfaction.
If you’d like, you can also send a short review here.

---

## Intake Rules
- Start short.
- Ask for photos only when useful.
- Avoid asking too many questions at once.
- Do not give final pricing before understanding the scope.
- Keep every response respectful and practical.
- Prefer WhatsApp for speed and simplicity.
