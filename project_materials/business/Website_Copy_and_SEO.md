# Website Copy and SEO

## Purpose
This file contains the current website messaging direction and SEO logic for CleanFixHarish.
It should guide homepage copy, service descriptions, metadata, and future page structure.

## Website Goal
The first website is a simple premium bilingual landing page / PWA built to:
- create trust quickly
- explain the services clearly
- push handyman strongly
- encourage fast WhatsApp contact
- generate quote requests

## Primary Conversion Goals
1. WhatsApp contact
2. Phone call
3. Quote form submission

---

## Homepage Positioning
CleanFixHarish should be presented as a premium local service in Harish.
The homepage must communicate this in seconds.

### Core homepage message
Premium home services in Harish, with strong handyman support and reliable cleaning care.

### Main homepage priorities
- clear value in one glance
- local trust
- premium simplicity
- handyman-first service emphasis
- fast contact path

---

## Homepage Section Order
1. Hero
2. Services overview
3. Why choose us / trust section
4. Quote / contact section
5. Footer with local contact information

## Trust Section Direction
The trust section should focus on realism, not hype.

### Example trust points
- Local Harish service
- Fast WhatsApp response
- Fair pricing
- Respectful work style
- Clean, practical, reliable help

---

## CTA Direction
Use short direct CTAs.

### Preferred CTA examples
- WhatsApp us now
- Request a quote
- Call now
- Send details
- Get a fast reply

### Avoid CTAs like
- Unlock your home’s potential
- Transform your living journey
- Experience excellence today

## SEO Direction
The first SEO strategy should stay local and simple.

### Primary keyword ideas
- handyman Harish
- handyman services Harish
- cleaning services Harish
- post renovation cleaning Harish
- move in cleaning Harish
- AC cleaning Harish
- window cleaning Harish
- home services Harish

### Hebrew keyword ideas
- הנדימן חריש
- תיקונים קטנים חריש
- ניקיון אחרי שיפוץ חריש
- ניקיון דירה חריש
- ניקוי מזגן חריש
- ניקוי חלונות חריש
- שירותי בית חריש

### SEO rules
- keep language natural
- do not keyword stuff
- connect keywords to real service pages or sections
- prioritize local clarity over volume chasing
- include Harish visibly in title, hero, and contact areas

---

## Future Page Expansion
When the business grows, the website can expand into:
- dedicated handyman page
- post-renovation cleaning page
- move-in / move-out cleaning page
- about / trust page
- local service area page
- FAQ page

Do not build all pages immediately unless there is a clear reason.

---

## Writing Rules for Website Content
- keep paragraphs short
- use clear subheadings
- avoid hype
- keep bilingual presentation readable
- always lead with clarity
- explain what the service is, for whom, and why it matters
- maintain premium tone without sounding expensive for the sake of it
