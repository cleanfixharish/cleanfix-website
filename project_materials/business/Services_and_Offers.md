# Services and Offers

## Purpose
This document defines the services, positioning, and offer structure for CleanFixHarish.
It should be used to keep service presentation consistent across website copy, ads, WhatsApp, and GPT outputs.

## Service Structure Overview
CleanFixHarish has one main service pillar and several supporting service pillars.

### Main growth pillar
- Handyman services

### Supporting pillars
- Post-renovation cleaning
- Move-in / move-out cleaning
- AC cleaning
- Window cleaning

---

## 1. Handyman Services
### Position
This is the lead service and should be pushed strongly in marketing, website structure, and customer communication.

### Description
Practical in-home help for small repairs, installations, and finishing work.
This service is designed for customers who want precise, reliable help without dealing with a full contractor or a messy renovation process.

### Example tasks
- shelf installation
- curtain rod installation
- TV mounting, if offered
- light fixture installation
- door handle fixes
- cabinet adjustments
- minor drywall or gypsum fixes
- touch-up work
- general small apartment repair jobs

### Customer value
- fast practical help
- less stress
- clean work style
- no need for a big renovation process
- useful for busy people and apartment residents

### Messaging angle
Small jobs done properly.

---

## 2. Post-Renovation Cleaning
### Position
A premium finishing service for homes that need to feel complete after renovation or repair work.

### Description
Deep cleaning after renovation, dust removal, surface cleaning, floor care, bathroom and kitchen reset, and detailed preparation for move-in or use.

### Customer value
- makes the home feel complete
- removes renovation residue and stress
- improves the final impression of the property

### Messaging angle
From finished work to a finished home.

---

## 3. Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning
### Position
A practical transition service for people entering or leaving an apartment.

### Description
Cleaning before moving in, before handing over a flat, or between tenants and owners.
Designed to create a fresh reset moment.

### Customer value
- cleaner handover
- calmer moving experience
- better first impression

### Messaging angle
Start clean. Leave well.

---

## 4. AC Cleaning
### Position
A simple but valuable comfort and freshness service.

### Description
Cleaning AC filters and core visible unit areas to improve air freshness and home comfort.

### Customer value
- fresher feeling air
- cleaner home environment
- useful seasonal maintenance

### Messaging angle
A fresher home starts with the air.

---

## 5. Window Cleaning
### Position
A finishing service that improves brightness and visual cleanliness.

### Description
Cleaning windows, frames, and tracks to help the home feel lighter, brighter, and more polished.

### Customer value
- clearer view
- brighter home
- better finishing detail

### Messaging angle
Clean windows change the whole room.

---

## Offer Strategy
The offer structure should stay simple in the first version of the business.
Do not create too many packages too early.

## Recommended Offer Presentation
### Website level
Present services in this order:
1. Handyman
2. Post-renovation cleaning
3. Move-in / move-out cleaning
4. AC cleaning
5. Window cleaning

### Lead capture level
Ask users what they need first, then guide them to the appropriate next step.

### Quote level
Use quote-based pricing for most services rather than rigid public package pricing.

---

## Bundling Ideas
These bundles are optional and should only be introduced when useful.

### Apartment Reset
- Handyman touch-ups
- move-in / move-out cleaning
- window cleaning

### After Work Finish
- minor handyman fixes
- post-renovation cleaning
- window cleaning

### Fresh Home Care
- AC cleaning
- window cleaning
- small home adjustment tasks

---

## Offer Rules
- Keep first offers simple.
- Emphasize handyman strongly.
- Use cleaning and support services to increase trust and convenience.
- Avoid confusing package names.
- Avoid fake “premium” language with no practical meaning.
- Always connect the offer to a real customer need.
