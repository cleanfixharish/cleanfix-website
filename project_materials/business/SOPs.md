# Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

## Purpose
This document defines early-stage operational standards for CleanFixHarish.
The goal is to create repeatable, clear service delivery systems that can be improved over time.

## Operating Principles
- be on time where possible
- communicate clearly
- work neatly
- respect the customer’s home
- leave the place in better condition than expected
- document repeatable work
- reduce chaos through checklists and habits

---

## SOP 1: New Lead Handling
1. Receive lead from WhatsApp, phone, or website form.
2. Confirm service type.
3. Confirm location.
4. Request photos if relevant.
5. Confirm timing or urgency.
6. Decide whether enough information exists for a quote or visit.
7. Reply clearly with next step.

### Quality standard
The customer should never feel ignored or confused about what happens next.

---

## SOP 2: Handyman Job Intake
1. Confirm exact task.
2. Ask for clear photos or measurements if relevant.
3. Confirm whether tools or materials are needed.
4. Confirm access details.
5. Give realistic quote or next step.
6. Confirm date and time if booked.

### Quality standard
No vague assumptions.
Understand the job before committing.

---

## SOP 3: Cleaning Job Intake
1. Confirm cleaning type.
2. Ask if property is post-renovation, move-in, or move-out.
3. Ask approximate apartment size if relevant.
4. Request photos if useful.
5. Confirm timing.
6. Provide next step or quote.

### Quality standard
Set realistic expectations before the job starts.

---

## SOP 4: Before Arrival
1. Confirm appointment in advance.
2. Check that required tools or supplies are ready.
3. Review the scope of work.
4. Prepare for arrival on time.
5. Update the customer if delayed.

### Quality standard
No surprises without communication.

---

## SOP 5: On-Site Behavior
1. Greet respectfully.
2. Confirm the task before starting.
3. Work neatly.
4. Protect surfaces where relevant.
5. Keep unnecessary mess to a minimum.
6. Communicate if something changes.

### Quality standard
The customer should feel their home is being treated with care.

---

## SOP 6: Job Completion
1. Review the finished work.
2. Confirm the customer is satisfied.
3. Clean up the immediate work area.
4. Explain any follow-up if needed.
5. Close the job politely.

### Quality standard
The finish should feel complete, not abandoned.

---

## SOP 7: Post-Service Follow-Up
1. Send a short thank-you message.
2. Ask whether everything was satisfactory.
3. Request a review only if appropriate.
4. Note any issues that need correction.

### Quality standard
The customer should feel remembered, not processed.

---

## SOP 8: Internal Improvement Loop
1. Review recurring issues.
2. Document better handling methods.
3. Update SOPs over time.
4. Convert successful responses into templates.

### Quality standard
Every repeated task should become easier and more consistent over time.
