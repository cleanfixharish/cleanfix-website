# Pricing Philosophy

## Purpose
This document defines how CleanFixHarish should think about pricing and how pricing should be communicated.
The goal is to stay fair, premium, and trustworthy without looking confusing or cheap.

## Core Pricing Position
CleanFixHarish is not a bargain-basement brand.
It is a premium but accessible local service.
That means pricing should feel fair, calm, and justified by quality, responsiveness, and clarity.

## Pricing Principles
- fair
- transparent
- realistic
- scope-based
- respectful
- not manipulative

## What Pricing Should Communicate
- this is serious service
- this is not random pricing
- this is not fake luxury
- this is not cheap-looking desperation
- this is worth paying for because it is clear and reliable

## Recommended Early-Stage Pricing Model
Use quote-based pricing rather than complex public rate cards.
This is especially useful for:
- handyman jobs with variable scope
- post-renovation cleaning
- move-in / move-out cleaning
- mixed service requests

## Why Quote-Based Pricing Fits
- jobs vary
- apartment sizes vary
- condition varies
- tool or supply needs may vary
- it reduces confusion and overpromising

## Communication Rules
- do not promise a final price before understanding the job
- do not bait with unrealistic starting prices
- do not create fake discounts
- do not overload the customer with too many pricing options
- keep explanations simple

## Good Pricing Language
- We can give you a quote after a few details.
- Please send a photo so we can understand the work properly.
- Pricing depends on the scope, and we try to keep it fair and clear.
- We’ll review the details and get back to you with the next step.

## Avoid Pricing Language Like
- Lowest price guaranteed
- Luxury package experience
- Exclusive elite tier
- Unbeatable deals
- Special secret offer

## Strategic Rule
Trust is more important than sounding cheap.
Clear pricing logic is more important than pretending every job fits a fixed package.

## Future Expansion
Later, the business may define:
- simple minimum visit logic
- clearer service categories
- bundled package examples
- repeat customer pricing policies

Do not overcomplicate this until volume requires it.
