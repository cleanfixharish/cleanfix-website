# Customer FAQ

## Purpose
This FAQ gives CleanFixHarish a simple, clear base for customer-facing answers.
It should be used in website copy, WhatsApp responses, and customer support templates.

## FAQ

### What services do you offer?
CleanFixHarish offers handyman services, post-renovation cleaning, move-in and move-out cleaning, AC cleaning, and window cleaning in Harish and nearby areas.

### Do you work in Harish only?
Harish is the main focus.
Nearby areas may also be available depending on the job and schedule.

### What kind of handyman work do you do?
We focus on practical small repairs, installations, shelf mounting, curtain rods, lighting, minor adjustments, and similar apartment help tasks.

### Do you do big renovations?
The main focus is practical smaller work and supportive home services.
Large renovations should only be discussed if clearly relevant and realistic.

### How do I get a quote?
The simplest way is to send a WhatsApp message with the service you need, your area, and photos if relevant.
That helps us understand the work and reply clearly.

### Do I need to send photos?
Not always, but photos are very helpful for handyman work, cleaning after renovation, and jobs where the condition matters.

### How quickly do you reply?
The goal is to reply as quickly as possible through WhatsApp or phone.

### Do you offer cleaning after renovation?
Yes.
Post-renovation cleaning is one of the core services.

### Do you offer move-in or move-out cleaning?
Yes.
This is part of the main service offering.

### Do you clean air conditioners?
Yes.
We offer practical AC cleaning to help improve air freshness and home comfort.

### Do you clean windows?
Yes.
We offer window cleaning including frames and tracks where relevant.

### How is pricing decided?
Pricing usually depends on the scope of work.
We try to keep pricing fair, clear, and based on the actual job.

### Are you a cheap service?
The business is designed to be fair and accessible, but not cheap-looking or low-quality.
The focus is value, clarity, and good work.

### How do I book?
Send a WhatsApp message or fill in the contact form with your details and service request.
We will review it and respond with the next step.

### Do you work in both Hebrew and English?
Yes.
CleanFixHarish communicates in both Hebrew and English.
