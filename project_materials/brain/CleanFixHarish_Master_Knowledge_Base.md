# CleanFixHarish Master Knowledge Base

## Company Overview
CleanFixHarish is a premium local home services business focused on Harish, Israel, and nearby areas.
The company is being built to feel calm, trustworthy, elegant, local, and highly practical.
It should feel more premium than a typical local service business, but still simple and easy to understand.

The company is designed around a modern local-service model:
- simple customer contact
- fast WhatsApp response
- fair pricing
- careful, reliable service
- bilingual communication in Hebrew and English
- scalable systems powered by GPTs and structured business knowledge

---

## Core Brand Position
CleanFixHarish is not trying to look cheap, noisy, or mass-market.
It is positioned as a premium but accessible local service brand.

The feeling should be:
- clean
- precise
- local
- elegant
- trustworthy
- simple
- calm

The brand inspiration is the emotional restraint and premium confidence associated with Rolls-Royce website design, adapted for a practical home services business.
That means the brand should feel high-end without being flashy.

---

## Geographic Focus
Primary service area:
- Harish, Israel

Secondary service area:
- nearby areas around Harish, based on practical travel and service availability

Location strategy:
- local-first positioning
- fast response in Harish
- nearby-area expansion only when operationally realistic

## Primary Services
### 1. Handyman Services
This is the main service pillar and should be pushed strongly in messaging and visual presentation.

Includes examples such as:
- shelf installation
- curtain installation
- light fixture installation
- minor door fixes
- minor cabinet fixes
- small drywall or gypsum work
- apartment touch-ups
- small practical repairs
- general home help tasks

Brand meaning:
This service should feel precise, clean, useful, and confidence-building.
It is the strongest wedge for customer acquisition.

### 2. Post-Renovation Cleaning
Includes:
- deep cleaning after renovation
- dust removal
- paint residue cleanup
- kitchen and bathroom finishing clean
- floor washing and final preparation

Brand meaning:
The home should feel complete, fresh, and ready.

### 3. Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning
Includes:
- cleaning before entering a new apartment
- cleaning before handing over an apartment
- whole-home reset cleaning

Brand meaning:
The apartment should feel prepared, clean, respectful, and ready for transition.

### 4. AC Cleaning
Includes:
- filter cleaning
- improving air freshness
- maintaining home comfort

Brand meaning:
Freshness, air quality, and quiet maintenance.

### 5. Window Cleaning
Includes:
- glass cleaning
- frame cleaning
- track cleaning

Brand meaning:
Brightness, clarity, and a polished home environment.

## Business Priorities
1. Build trust quickly.
2. Make handyman service a primary growth driver.
3. Keep the website simple and conversion-focused.
4. Use WhatsApp as a main contact path.
5. Keep operations lean and affordable.
6. Build a premium reputation gradually through consistency.
7. Avoid overbuilding too early.
8. Scale through systems, not chaos.

Essential sections:

## Tone of Voice
The tone should be:
- calm
- respectful
- elegant
- direct
- practical
- trustworthy
- warm but not casual
- premium but not pretentious

Avoid:
- hype
- exaggerated claims
- fake authority
- loud advertising language
- generic AI-sounding phrases
- overly technical jargon for customers

Examples of phrases to avoid:
- revolutionizing home services
- all-in-one solution
- unlock the power of
- premium solutions for every need
- world-class service experience

Preferred style:
- short clear sentences
- useful information
- confidence without drama
- local realism
- honest promises

---

## Language Rules
CleanFixHarish communicates in both Hebrew and English.

Language standards:
- Hebrew should be natural, modern, and easy to understand.
- English should be clear, polished, and simple.
- If content is customer-facing, Hebrew often comes first unless the context requires otherwise.
- Bilingual layouts should remain visually clean and not overcrowded.
- Never use awkward machine-style translation.

---

## Visual Identity Direction
The visual direction should communicate premium calm.

### Visual personality
- minimal
- elegant
- quiet confidence
- luxury restraint
- warm and refined
- polished, not flashy

### Style inspiration
- Rolls-Royce website feeling
- premium editorial interiors
- warm neutral spaces
- clean composition
- expensive simplicity

### Color direction
Default palette direction:
- warm beige
- soft ivory
- stone
- charcoal
- deep teal accent

The brand should use mostly neutrals with restrained accent color use.

### Visual rules
- avoid clutter
- avoid bright plastic colors
- avoid neon or aggressive gradients
- avoid cartoonish service imagery
- avoid low-trust generic stock style
- prioritize realistic premium imagery
- use negative space intentionally
- make handyman visuals feel precise and curated, not rough or messy

### Logo direction
The logo should be:
- simple
- modern
- scalable
- suitable for website, favicon, and future app icon use
- bilingual or supported by bilingual lockups
- understated, not decorative

## Pricing Philosophy
The brand should feel premium but still fair.

Pricing principles:
- fair and transparent
- no misleading low-price baiting
- no exaggerated package names unless genuinely useful
- quote based on work scope
- practical first, complex later
- trust and clarity matter more than fake discounts

Pricing communication should avoid sounding cheap, desperate, or confusing.

---

## Customer Experience Standards
The customer experience should feel smooth and respectful.

### Before the job
- quick response
- simple intake questions
- clear next step
- realistic scope discussion

### During the job
- respectful communication
- clean work habits
- practical updates if needed
- professional behavior

### After the job
- clear completion
- optional follow-up
- ask for review only when appropriate
- maintain trust and simplicity

---

## WhatsApp and Lead Flow Strategy
WhatsApp is a primary communication channel.

Lead flow should be simple:
1. customer sees website or ad
2. customer sends WhatsApp or fills short form
3. business collects basic details
4. quote or next step is provided quickly
5. job is scheduled if relevant

Minimum intake data:
- name
- phone
- area
- service needed
- timing
- notes or photos if necessary

The first response should be short, polite, and practical.

---

## Operational Philosophy
The business should scale by systems and clear standards.

That means:
- SOPs should be documented
- task checklists should be reused
- GPTs should be role-specific
- no overlapping responsibility unless clearly intended
- every recurring task should become a documented process over time

---

## AI Team Philosophy
The AI system is part of the business infrastructure.
It should help CleanFixHarish become more organized, more premium, and more scalable.

### Goals of the AI team
- improve consistency
- reduce decision fatigue
- accelerate content and asset creation
- organize internal knowledge
- create reusable business systems
- strengthen the premium brand across all touchpoints

### AI team rules
- each GPT has one main responsibility
- GPTs must not overlap unnecessarily
- project files are the shared source of truth
- every GPT should respect the brand and tone rules in this knowledge base

---

## Non-Negotiables
These rules should be respected across all GPTs and customer-facing outputs.

- Do not fabricate reviews.
- Do not make unrealistic promises.
- Do not use generic hype language.
- Do not dilute the premium brand with messy visuals or copy.
- Do not overcomplicate the first version of the business.
- Do not treat every service equally in brand emphasis; handyman should be pushed strongly.
- Do not create overlap between GPT roles when clarity is possible.

---

## Current Strategic Direction
At the current stage, the company is focused on:
- launching a simple premium bilingual website/PWA
- using AI-generated images and brand assets
- creating a small but well-structured GPT workforce
- building trust in Harish first
- using handyman service as the main marketing edge
- adding complexity only when it improves results
