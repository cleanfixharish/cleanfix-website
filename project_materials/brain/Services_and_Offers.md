# CleanFixHarish Services and Offers

> The definitive list of what CleanFixHarish offers, how each service is positioned,
> and how to talk about it. Use this for website copy, service pages, ads, and WhatsApp.
> Pricing here is philosophy and structure only — never quote fixed prices without scope.

---

## Service Emphasis (important)

Not all services are marketed equally. **Handyman services are the primary growth wedge**
and should be pushed most strongly in messaging and visuals. The cleaning services are
strong, trust-building supporting pillars.

Priority order for marketing emphasis:
1. Handyman services (lead driver)
2. Post-renovation cleaning
3. Move-in / move-out cleaning
4. AC cleaning
5. Window cleaning

---

## 1. Handyman Services — PRIMARY

**What it is:** Practical small repairs, installations, and general home help.

**Examples:**
- Shelf installation
- Curtain / curtain-rod installation
- Light fixture installation
- Minor door fixes
- Minor cabinet fixes
- Small drywall / gypsum work
- Apartment touch-ups
- Small practical repairs
- General home help tasks

**Not this:** Large renovations. Only discuss big jobs if clearly relevant and realistic.

**Brand meaning:** precise, clean, useful, confidence-building. This is the strongest
wedge for customer acquisition.

**How to talk about it:** "Small home repairs and installations, done carefully and on
time." Emphasize precision, reliability, and quick help.

---

## 2. Post-Renovation Cleaning

**What it is:** Deep cleaning after renovation work.

**Includes:** dust removal, paint-residue cleanup, kitchen and bathroom finishing clean,
floor washing and final preparation.

**Brand meaning:** the home should feel complete, fresh, and ready.

**How to talk about it:** "We make a freshly renovated home move-in ready — spotless,
detailed, and calm."

---

## 3. Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning

**What it is:** Whole-home reset cleaning around a move.

**Includes:** cleaning before entering a new apartment, cleaning before handing over an
apartment, full-home reset.

**Brand meaning:** the apartment should feel prepared, clean, respectful, ready for transition.

**How to talk about it:** "Start fresh, or hand over with confidence — a complete home reset."

---

## 4. AC Cleaning

**What it is:** Practical air-conditioner cleaning and maintenance.

**Includes:** filter cleaning, improving air freshness, home-comfort maintenance.

**Brand meaning:** freshness, air quality, quiet maintenance.

**How to talk about it:** "Cleaner air, better comfort — simple AC cleaning and upkeep."

---

## 5. Window Cleaning

**What it is:** Glass, frames, and tracks.

**Includes:** glass cleaning, frame cleaning, track cleaning.

**Brand meaning:** brightness, clarity, a polished home environment.

**How to talk about it:** "Brighter rooms, clearer views — windows cleaned inside and out."

## Pricing Philosophy

Premium but fair. The brand should never look cheap, desperate, or confusing.

Principles:
- Fair and transparent.
- No misleading low-price baiting.
- No exaggerated package names unless genuinely useful.
- Quote based on the actual scope of work.
- Practical first, complex later.
- Trust and clarity matter more than fake discounts.

**Rule for all outputs:** Never promise an exact price before enough job detail is known.
Ask for scope (and photos where helpful), then give a clear next step.

---

## How to Structure an Offer / Service Page

For each service, use this simple structure:
1. **Short clear title** (name of service).
2. **One-line promise** (the brand-meaning line above, adapted).
3. **What's included** (3–6 plain bullets).
4. **Who it's for / when to use it.**
5. **Clear next step:** WhatsApp CTA + short quote-request form.

Keep it calm and honest. Lead with usefulness, not adjectives.

---

## Trust Signals (use honestly)

- Local and responsive in Harish.
- Careful, detail-focused work.
- Fair, scope-based pricing.
- Bilingual, respectful communication.
- Clear process from first message to completed job.

Never fabricate reviews, counts, or credentials. Only use trust signals that are true.
