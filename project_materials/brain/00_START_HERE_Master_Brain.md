## The business in one paragraph

CleanFixHarish is a premium-feeling but practical local home-services business in
Harish, Israel (and nearby areas). Its main growth wedge is **handyman services**,
supported by post-renovation cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, AC cleaning, and
window cleaning. It is trust-first, fair-price, quality-first, bilingual (Hebrew +
English, with English NOT treated as secondary), and WhatsApp-first for leads. The
brand aesthetic is "Rolls-Royce feel" — calm, elegant, quiet confidence, never loud
or salesy. The business runs on a small, well-structured team of specialist GPTs
built on the OpenAI platform, all sharing this knowledge base as the source of truth.

- **Known open decision:** Whether to stay on Atoms or migrate the app + data onto the
  owner's own Supabase + Vercel + Cloudflare stack for full ownership/control.

## Non-negotiables (apply to every output)

- No fake reviews, no fake claims, no exaggerated promises.
- No generic hype or AI-sounding language ("revolutionizing", "unlock the power of", etc.).
- Keep the premium brand clean — no messy visuals or cluttered copy.
- Don't overcomplicate the first version.
- Push handyman as the main marketing edge.
- Bilingual: Hebrew natural and local, English clear and polished. English is not secondary.
- Owner is non-technical for operations — keep systems simple to run.
- Inform of costs and ask for confirmation before cost-incurring actions.
