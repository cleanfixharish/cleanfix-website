# CleanFixHarish Visual System

> The complete visual identity for CleanFixHarish, plus a reusable, step-by-step
> image-generation prompt sequence. All designs follow the golden ratio (1:1.618).
> Use this with any image model (ChatGPT image tools, Midjourney, etc.).

---

## Brand Personality

CleanFixHarish should feel **dependable, premium, calm, precise, and local**. The
visual identity communicates that every detail is noticed and every home is treated
with respect. The brand avoids loud marketing aesthetics and instead projects quiet
confidence, cleanliness, and professionalism.

---

## Logo Direction

Modern, minimal, highly legible.

- Clean geometric typography with slightly rounded edges (approachable).
- A subtle reference to care, precision, or home services — without becoming generic.
- Minimal symbols: a refined check mark, a simplified house outline, a subtle sparkle,
  or an abstract cleaning motion integrated into the typography.
- Colors: primary soft professional blue; secondary fresh green accent; neutral warm
  gray and white support.
- Must work on: white backgrounds, vehicles, uniforms, invoices, WhatsApp profile
  images, and website navigation bars.
- Feeling: somewhere between healthcare cleanliness and premium home services.

Note: The Master Knowledge Base also describes an alternate premium neutral palette
(warm beige, ivory, stone, charcoal, deep teal accent). Treat blue/green as the
"clean & fresh" direction and warm-neutral/teal as the "premium editorial" direction;
pick one lockup and stay consistent across all assets.

---

## Photography Style

Photography is the core of the visual language.

- Photorealistic imagery only.
- Bright natural daylight; soft shadows with high visibility and clarity.
- Clean white interiors with subtle warmth.
- Real Israeli homes and small offices — not luxury mansions.
- Visible attention to detail and organization.
- Authentic, professional human presence — not posed.

Preferred subjects: carefully cleaned kitchens, fresh bathrooms, organized living
rooms, bright office desks, professional technicians performing meticulous work,
small details that communicate care and precision.

Avoid: overly staged stock photography, artificial HDR, excessive contrast, dark
interiors, heavy color grading, models smiling directly at the camera unnecessarily.

Images should feel quiet, fresh, and trustworthy.

---

## Icon Style

Minimal flat design language.

- Rounded corners, simple geometry, consistent line thickness, soft color fills,
  minimal detail.
- Categories: cleaning services, handyman services, AC cleaning, move-in/move-out
  cleaning, window cleaning, offices and apartments, booking/scheduling.
- Color: primary blue for structure, fresh green for confirmations/positive actions,
  warm neutral gray for supporting information.
- Must remain readable at very small sizes.

---

## Color System

Primary: Clean White, Soft Sky Blue, Fresh Sage Green.
Supporting: Warm Beige, Light Stone Gray, Soft Silver.

- Blue = trust and professionalism.
- Green = freshness and hygiene.
- Warm neutrals prevent the interface from feeling clinical.
- Keep colors soft and restrained.

---

## Golden Ratio Implementation

**Hero sections** — 1:1.618 composition. Headline near a golden-ratio intersection;
supporting text ~38% of horizontal space; hero image ~62%; primary CTA near the lower
golden-ratio line. Eye flow: Headline → Value Proposition → Action Button → Image.

**Service cards** — icon/image area ~38%, text/CTA area ~62%. Internal spacing (title,
description, button) follows the same relationship. Balanced without feeling rigid.

**Banners** — place the visual subject near a golden-ratio intersection (not centered).
Headline on the opposite side; supporting info on secondary ratio lines.

---

## Consistency Rules

Every asset shares: identical lighting philosophy, identical color temperature,
identical blue/green values, identical cleanliness standards, identical emotional tone.
The whole site should feel as if every image was photographed on the same morning, in
the same city, by the same photographer, for the same family of services.

Final impression: Reliable. Precise. Clean. Local. Professional. Calm.

# Step-by-Step Image Generation Prompts

Use each block below as a separate prompt, in order.

## Step 1 — Establish the visual system (run first)

```text
You are my brand visual designer and image generator.

I will describe the visual system for a cleaning + handyman company called
"CleanFixHarish". Your job: (1) confirm you understand the system, (2) follow it
strictly in every image you generate.

Visual system:
- Brand personality: dependable, premium, calm, precise, local. Avoid loud, salesy
  aesthetics; focus on quiet confidence, cleanliness, and professionalism.
- Logo direction: modern, minimal, highly legible; clean geometric typography with
  slightly rounded edges; very subtle home/care/precision reference; minimal symbols
  (check mark, simple house outline, subtle sparkle, abstract cleaning motion).
- Colors: primary soft professional blue; secondary fresh green accent; neutrals warm
  gray and white. Blue = trust/professional; green = freshness/hygiene; warm neutrals
  keep it from feeling clinical.
- Photography: photorealistic only; bright natural daylight; soft shadows; clean white
  interiors with subtle warmth; real Israeli homes and small offices (not luxury
  mansions); authentic professional human presence; visible attention to detail. Avoid
  heavy staging, HDR, dark interiors, heavy grading, cheesy stock poses.
- Icons: minimal flat design; rounded corners; simple geometry; consistent line
  thickness; soft fills; minimal detail; readable at small sizes.
- Color system: Clean White, Soft Sky Blue, Fresh Sage Green, plus Warm Beige, Light
  Stone Gray, Soft Silver as supporting colors; used softly and restrained.
- Golden ratio:
  - Hero sections: layout 1:1.618; headline near golden-ratio intersection; text ~38%;
    image ~62%; main CTA on lower golden-ratio line.
  - Service cards: icon/image ~38%, text/CTA ~62%; spacing and button placement follow
    same ratio.
  - Banners: main subject near golden-ratio intersection, headline on opposite side,
    secondary info on secondary ratio lines.
- Consistency: identical lighting, color temperature, blue/green values, cleanliness
  level, emotional tone across all assets; everything should feel shot in the same
  morning, same city, same photographer.

First, respond with a short checklist summarizing how you'll enforce this system in
every image. Do not generate images yet.
```

## Step 2 — Logo (4 options, transparent PNG)

```text
Design the core logo for "CleanFixHarish" using the visual system you just acknowledged.

Requirements:
- Transparent background PNG.
- Modern, minimal, highly legible wordmark; clean geometric typography with slightly
  rounded edges.
- Subtle symbol referencing home care and precision (refined check mark + simplified
  house outline + subtle sparkle), integrated or aligned with the text.
- Colors: soft professional blue main, fresh green accent, warm gray support.
- Golden ratio: overall footprint respects 1:1.618; symbol height and wordmark width
  follow golden-ratio proportions; spacing between symbol and text based on 1 and 1.618 units.
- Must work on white, vehicles, uniforms, invoices, WhatsApp profile, and site nav.

Generate 4 transparent PNG logos:
- A: horizontal (symbol left, text right).
- B: stacked (symbol above, text below).
- C: badge variant for WhatsApp/profile use.
- D: minimal wordmark with a very subtle cleaning accent.

For each variant, briefly describe where the golden ratio is applied.
```

## Step 3 — Homepage hero image

```text
Generate a homepage hero image for the CleanFixHarish website, strictly following the
visual system.

Scene: bright, freshly cleaned living room or kitchen in a real Israeli home (not luxury
mansion); daylight from windows, soft shadows, slightly warm tone; organized, precise,
cared for.

Composition (golden ratio): 16:9 horizontal frame; main focal area around a golden-ratio
intersection; ~62% of width imagery, ~38% cleaner area for a headline + button (negative
space, still consistent with the scene); CTA area near the lower golden-ratio line.

Style: photorealistic, high resolution; described lighting and color system; no staged
stock look, no HDR, no harsh contrast.

Generate 3 variations with slightly different rooms/angles but identical mood, color
temperature, and cleanliness level.
```

## Step 4 — Service card images (repeat per category)

```text
Create a 4:3 service card image for the CleanFixHarish website, following the visual system.

Service category: [deep home cleaning / office cleaning / move-in & move-out cleaning /
post-renovation cleaning / window cleaning / carpet & upholstery cleaning /
air-conditioning cleaning / handyman services].

Scene: realistic Israeli home or small-office context; a professional cleaner or a precise
cleaning/handyman detail that clearly expresses this service; visible order, attention to
detail, calm atmosphere.

Composition (golden ratio): ~38% icon/imagery zone, ~62% text/CTA zone; main subject around
a golden-ratio line; clean negative space in the text zone for title, short description, button.

Style: photorealistic; same lighting, color temperature, blue/green values, and cleanliness
as the hero image.

Generate 2 variations with consistent palette and mood.
```

## Step 5 — About / team image

```text
Generate a horizontal 3:2 "About CleanFixHarish" image.

Scene: small local team (3–4 professionals) in branded shirts using the CleanFixHarish logo
and colors; clean, modest Israeli interior (office or hallway); bright natural daylight;
authentic, relaxed professional stance (not exaggerated smiles or staged corporate feel).

Composition (golden ratio): group occupies a golden-ratio rectangle (~62% width), leaving
~38% clean space on one side for a text block.

Style: photorealistic; same morning-like light and emotional tone as all other images.
```

## Step 6 — Testimonials background

```text
Generate a soft background image for the testimonials section.

Requirements: abstract / very subtle out-of-focus interior shapes using Clean White, Soft
Sky Blue, Fresh Sage Green, and warm neutrals; minimal visual noise so text stays readable;
gentle suggestion of cleanliness (soft sparkle shapes or faint geometric motifs).

Composition (golden ratio): 16:9; subtle focal accent on a golden-ratio intersection, rest
intentionally quiet.

Style: consistent with the visual system; calm, premium, clean, trustworthy.
```

## Step 7 — Booking CTA banner

```text
Generate a "Book CleanFixHarish" call-to-action banner.

Scene: clean entrance or hallway of an Israeli home, slightly open door suggesting welcoming
cleanliness; bright daylight, soft shadows, tidy and precise environment.

Composition (golden ratio): 16:9; door and main focal point at a golden-ratio intersection;
reserve ~38% of the frame as cleaner area for the main headline and button.

Style: photorealistic; same lighting, palette, and emotional tone; premium and calm, not pushy.
```

## Step 8 — Icon set

```text
Design a flat icon set for CleanFixHarish using the visual and color system.

Icons: deep home cleaning, office cleaning, air-conditioning cleaning, move-in/move-out
cleaning, window cleaning, handyman services, booking/scheduling, satisfaction guarantee.

Characteristics: minimal flat design; rounded corners; simple geometry; consistent line
thickness; soft fills (primary blue for structure, fresh green for positive actions, warm
gray for supporting info); clear at very small sizes.

Golden ratio: build geometry with circles and rectangles proportioned using 1 and 1.618
units; apply golden-ratio spacing inside each icon.

Output icons on transparent backgrounds suitable for light or white interfaces.
```

## Step 9 — Consistency review (run periodically)

```text
Review the last 10 images created for CleanFixHarish and describe:
- how well they match the defined visual system,
- where lighting, color temperature, or golden-ratio composition drifted,
- what adjustments you'll make next batch to keep everything feeling like
  "same morning, same city, same photographer".
Then regenerate the one image that needs the most correction.
```

---

## Full generation sequence (summary)

1. Establish visual system → 2. Logo options → 3. Hero image → 4. Service category cards
(one per service) → 5. About/team photo → 6. Testimonials background → 7. Booking CTA
banner → 8. Icon set → 9. Consistency review. Keep palette, lighting, mood, and golden-ratio
composition identical throughout.
