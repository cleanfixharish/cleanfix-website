# CleanFixHarish Brand Guide

> The single reference for how CleanFixHarish looks, sounds, and feels.
> Every GPT and every asset (copy, visuals, ads, WhatsApp replies) must respect this guide.
> For detailed image direction and prompts, see `CleanFixHarish_Visual_System.md`.

---

## 1. Brand Essence

CleanFixHarish is a **premium-feeling but practical local home-services business** in
Harish, Israel, and nearby areas. It should feel more premium than a typical local
service business, while staying simple, honest, and easy to understand.

One-line positioning:
> "Trusted local handyman and home-care services in Harish — precise, fair, and calm."

Brand feeling (the six words): **clean, precise, local, elegant, trustworthy, calm.**

Inspiration: the emotional restraint and quiet confidence of Rolls-Royce digital
design — adapted for a real local service business. High-end without being flashy.

---

## 2. Brand Personality

- **Dependable** — we do what we say, on time, with care.
- **Premium** — refined, considered, never cheap-looking.
- **Calm** — no hype, no pressure, no noise.
- **Precise** — attention to detail is visible in the work and the brand.
- **Local** — genuinely of Harish, not a faceless national chain.

If the brand were a person: a calm, skilled, well-presented local professional who
takes pride in the details and never oversells.

---

## 3. Brand Values

1. **Trust first** — honesty over persuasion.
2. **Fair price** — transparent, scope-based, no bait pricing.
3. **Quality** — careful, reliable work.
4. **Clarity** — easy to understand and easy to contact.
5. **Local respect** — treat every home and customer with respect.
6. **Scalable simplicity** — grow through systems, not chaos.

---

## 4. Tone of Voice

**Sound like:** calm, respectful, elegant, direct, practical, trustworthy, warm but
not casual, premium but not pretentious.

**Write like this:**
- Short, clear sentences.
- Useful information over selling.
- Confidence without drama.
- Local realism.
- Honest promises only.

**Never sound like:** hype, exaggerated claims, fake authority, loud advertising,
generic AI phrasing, or over-technical jargon for customers.

**Banned phrases (examples):**
- "revolutionizing home services"
- "all-in-one solution"
- "unlock the power of"
- "premium solutions for every need"
- "world-class service experience"

**Quick before/after:**
- Weak: "We offer world-class, all-in-one home solutions!"
- Right: "Local handyman and home-cleaning help in Harish. Fair prices, careful work."

---

## 5. Language Rules (Bilingual)

CleanFixHarish communicates in **Hebrew and English**. English is **not** treated as
secondary — Harish has multiple language groups (Hebrew, English, Russian speakers).

- Hebrew: natural, modern, local, easy to understand. Never machine-style translation.
- English: clear, polished, simple.
- For customer-facing content, Hebrew often comes first, unless context requires otherwise.
- Bilingual layouts stay clean and uncrowded.
- Keep both languages equally cared-for in quality.

---

## 6. Color System

**Two consistent directions — pick one lockup per surface and stay consistent.**

**Direction A — "Clean & Fresh" (UI / icons / service imagery):**
- Primary: Soft Sky Blue (trust, professionalism)
- Secondary: Fresh Sage Green (freshness, hygiene)
- Neutrals: Clean White, Warm Beige, Light Stone Gray, Soft Silver

**Direction B — "Premium Editorial" (hero, brand, high-end feel):**
- Warm Beige, Soft Ivory, Stone, Charcoal
- Deep Teal accent

Rules: mostly neutrals, restrained accent use. No bright plastic colors, no neon, no
aggressive gradients.

---

## 7. Typography

- Clean, geometric, highly legible sans-serif with slightly rounded edges (approachable
  but modern).
- Generous spacing and clear hierarchy — let negative space do work.
- Bilingual pairing: choose a Hebrew face and a Latin face that share weight and feel so
  Hebrew and English look like one system.

---

## 8. Logo Direction

- Simple, modern, scalable; works as website logo, favicon, and future app icon.
- Subtle reference to home care / precision (refined check mark, simple house outline,
  subtle sparkle, or abstract cleaning motion) — never generic.
- Understated, not decorative.
- Must work on: white backgrounds, vehicles, uniforms, invoices, WhatsApp profile,
  and website navigation.
- Bilingual or supported by bilingual lockups.

Full logo prompt options are in `CleanFixHarish_Visual_System.md`.

---

## 9. Visual Identity (summary)

- Photorealistic imagery only; bright natural daylight, soft shadows, clean interiors
  with subtle warmth; real Israeli homes and small offices (not luxury mansions).
- Authentic professional presence; visible attention to detail.
- Minimal flat icons: rounded corners, simple geometry, consistent line thickness.
- **All designs follow the golden ratio (1:1.618)** — hero sections, cards, banners, icons.
- Everything should feel "shot on the same morning, same city, same photographer."

**Avoid:** clutter, cartoonish service imagery, cheesy stock poses, HDR, dark interiors,
heavy grading, low-trust generic stock style.

---

## 10. Do / Don't

**Do:**
- Push handyman as the main marketing edge.
- Keep the first version simple and conversion-focused.
- Use WhatsApp as the primary contact path.
- Stay bilingual and equally polished in both languages.
- Use negative space intentionally.

**Don't:**
- Fabricate reviews or testimonials.
- Make unrealistic promises or guarantees.
- Use hype or generic AI-sounding language.
- Overcomplicate the early business.
- Treat every service equally in emphasis (handyman leads).
- Dilute the premium feel with messy visuals or copy.

---

## 11. Brand Promise

CleanFixHarish should feel like the **premium local option** that is easy to understand,
easy to contact, visually consistent, well organized internally, and trusted because the
experience is calm, clear, and professional.
